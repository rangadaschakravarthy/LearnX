# Day 08 — DOM Traversal

## Overview
Welcome to **Day 08 — DOM Traversal**. This module teaches the fundamentals of parentElement & parentNode, children & childNodes, firstElementChild & lastElementChild, nextElementSibling & previousElementSibling, closest(), matches(), contains().

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this day, you will be able to:
- Understand and apply parentElement & parentNode
- Understand and apply children & childNodes
- Understand and apply firstElementChild & lastElementChild
- Understand and apply nextElementSibling & previousElementSibling
- Understand and apply closest()
- Understand and apply matches()
- Understand and apply contains()
