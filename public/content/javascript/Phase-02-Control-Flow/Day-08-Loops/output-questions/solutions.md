# Day 08 Output Prediction Solutions

### Question 1
```text
1
2
3
```
**Explanation:** `for` loop iterates from `i = 1` up to `i = 3` inclusive.

---

### Question 2
```text
3
```
**Explanation:** `count` increments from `0` to `1`, `2`, and `3`. When `count` reaches `3`, `3 < 3` evaluates to `false`, exiting the loop.

---

### Question 3
```text
1
2
```
**Explanation:** `break` executes at `i === 3`, terminating the loop immediately.

---

### Question 4
```text
1
2
4
5
```
**Explanation:** `continue` executes at `i === 3`, skipping `console.log(3)` and advancing directly to `i = 4`.

---

### Question 5
```text
18
```
**Explanation:** Trace calculation:
- `i=1`: `1*1 + 1*2 = 3`
- `i=2`: `2*1 + 2*2 = 6`
- `i=3`: `3*1 + 3*2 = 9`
- Total `sum = 3 + 6 + 9 = 18`.
